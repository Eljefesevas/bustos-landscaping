# Homepage hero: The Garden Reveal

Scope: the homepage hero only (`src/components/Hero.tsx` + its styles). Mode: Persuade. Rest of the homepage is untouched.

Audience: homeowners evaluating whether Bustos can transform their yard. Action: View Our Work (/our-work), Start Your Project (/contact). Proof available: none real; scene is a free-license Unsplash photograph (Gene Giromini, Carmel-by-the-Sea), labeled as concept imagery.

Direction pinned by the brief (Concept 1, The Garden Reveal); no roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hero is one real garden photograph that the visitor's scroll finishes. Not a split hero, not a before/after slider, not a crossfade between two images: the same yard, read as bare graded soil and flat stone, plants in and the light warms as you scroll.

OWN-WORLD: Photographic ground edge to edge. Palette is pulled from the scene: deep hedge green #1f2c22, sage #8d9a7f, warm stone #d9ccb4, off-white #f3efe6, charcoal #1a1d1a. Display set in Marcellus (inscriptional, stone-cut Roman) and UI in Hanken Grotesk. Hairline rules, square buttons, no cards, no glass.

STORY: Visitor sees an unfinished yard with potential, scrolls, watches beds fill and light turn golden, reads "Bringing Outdoor Spaces to Life", believes the company works with care, and taps View Our Work or Start Your Project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL canvas of the photo. Copy block bottom-left over a soft charcoal falloff, headline about 5.5rem on two lines, CTAs directly under the support line. Bottom-right shows a hairline progress rule labelled Site prepared → Planted → Finished, plus the concept-imagery credit. The headline is visible from the first frame.

FORM: Brief-pinned Garden Reveal; scroll-scrubbed WebGL grade and vegetation-growth shader over a pinned section; seed: none (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
